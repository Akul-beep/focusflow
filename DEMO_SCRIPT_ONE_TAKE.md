# FocusFlow — One-Take Demo Script (read this)

---

**Opening**

This is my AI task management app, FocusFlow.

**[Show code]**

For the front end I've used React and Tailwind. For the back end I've used Next.js and the Google Gemini API. This is an example prompt I've given to the Gemini API.

**[Back to the website]**

**Dashboard** is the home screen: stats, task list, filters, Growth Forest and daily motivation on the side, and **Start working**. The AI Task Creator in the sidebar breaks tasks into steps and schedules them.

**[Today]**

**Today** is the day view: what’s on for this date, quick recovery if plans slip, and a path into Focus.

**[Focus mode]**

**Focus** has the Pomodoro timer and Growth Forest. When a focus session ends, you choose whether to mark the step done so time logged stays honest—then you take a break.

**[Calendar]**

The Calendar shows classes and scheduled work and avoids overlaps when things change.

**[Notes, Exams, Analytics]**

Notes, Exams prep, and Analytics are in the main menu (on phone, Exams and Analytics also appear at the top of Settings).

**[Settings]**

Work window, session length, break length, optional morning briefing, personal goal, FocusCoins explanation, and Google sign-in for sync.

**[Wrap-up]**

FocusFlow uses AI to chunk work, respect your calendar, and make starting focus sessions straightforward.
